# Ethics and limitations (draft, expand as you build)

- The score is a proxy for perceived safety, not a crime forecast.
- NCRB publishes district-level counts only; street-level signals come from OSM tags,
  open POIs, transit proximity and any licence-compatible crowdsourced data.
- OSM tag coverage (for example `lit=*`) varies by area; low coverage is not low safety.
- Risk of harm: a map that labels neighbourhoods "unsafe" can stigmatise communities.
  Show route trade-offs and contributing factors, not neighbourhood verdicts.
- Document data sources, dates, known gaps, and how weights were chosen.
